Xiaolai's upstream Excalidraw version was subsetted to CJK, had Hangul side-bearings reduced to 40%, and had miscentered CJK codepoints centered. The plugin makes no additional font modifications.

**Liberation Sans is deliberately omitted from packaged font assets.** Excalidraw's binary is version 1.05, not modern OFL-licensed Liberation 2.x. Its historical redistribution agreement was not established. [Recorded metadata](./licenses/Liberation-METADATA.txt) is audit evidence only, not permission to distribute that font. Verify the historical agreement and obligations or obtain permission before adding it.
